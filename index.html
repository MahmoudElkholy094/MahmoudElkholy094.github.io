About

Background

Mahmoud Elkholy is a Postdoctoral Researcher at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM). His research focuses on renewable energy systems, microgrids, AI-driven energy management, and intelligent control of future power networks. He has collaborated internationally with researchers from Saudi Arabia, Japan, Egypt, and China.

He completed his Ph.D. in Integrated Intelligence Engineering at the University of the Ryukyus with an Outstanding grade (CGPA 4.0/4.0), earning the university’s President’s Honorary Award — its highest recognition for a graduate student. He is Co-Principal Investigator on two internationally funded research programs and serves as a reviewer for IEEE Access, Energy Conversion and Management, and other leading journals in the field.
